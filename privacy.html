Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Summary

Daily Chances does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, and no advertising.

What stays on your iPhone

Your daily rolls, points, goals, ratings, and tally are stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to any server. If you delete the app, that information is gone.

Analytics and tracking

Daily Chances does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services. The App Store itself may provide the developer with anonymous aggregate information (such as number of downloads and crash reports), as it does for every app on the store.

Children

Daily Chances is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
