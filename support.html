Common questions

How does it work?
Each morning, tap the dice to get an Action (Hello, Wave, Smile, Admire, Talk, or Serve) and a Person (Friend, Family, Contact, Stranger, Aged, or Eye‑to‑Eye). Say your challenge out loud three times, then look for the chance to do it during your day. When you do, tap "I Did It!" and collect your points.

Is it free?
Yes. There are no ads, no in‑app purchases, and no subscriptions.

Where is my information stored?
On your iPhone, and nowhere else. See the privacy policy.

I lost my tally after reinstalling. Can I get it back?
Because nothing is sent to a server, there is no backup to restore from. Starting fresh is the only option. (This is a trade‑off for keeping the app completely private.)
